# Policy approval register

Do not publish policy language from this register. It is a controlled checklist for counsel and clinical review.

| Document | Required owner | Required evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terms of Use | Healthcare counsel | Approved text, version, effective date and entity name | Not supplied |
| Privacy Notice | Privacy counsel | Approved text, covered entities, data flow, rights process and contact | Not supplied |
| Telehealth Consent | Healthcare counsel and medical group | Approved state-aware text, provider relationship, limitations and escalation instructions | Not supplied |
| Communications Consent | Privacy/marketing counsel | Separate transactional and optional marketing language | Not supplied |
| Notice of Privacy Practices, if applicable | Covered entity/privacy counsel | Approved applicability, responsible entity and acknowledgment process | Not supplied |
| Clinical protocols | Prescribing medical group | Signed field, branch, disposition and escalation approval | Not supplied |
| Pharmacy disclosures | Pharmacy partner and counsel | Approved fulfillment, compounding and substitution language | Not supplied |

Each approved document must receive a stable version, effective timestamp and SHA-256 content hash before acceptance can be recorded.
