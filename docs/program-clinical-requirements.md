# Program clinical requirements

These requirements are a review checklist, not clinical criteria. The prescribing medical group must supply and approve the actual questions, labels, branching and disposition rules.

## Weight Loss

- Verify applicable branded or compounded treatment pathways before creating medication-specific questions.
- Approve pregnancy, medical history, medication, allergy, procedure and prior GLP-1 questions.
- Approve whether BMI is used only as context or in any program rule.
- Validate all contraindication and escalation logic against the specific medication’s current approved labeling and the provider group’s protocols.
- Replace automated “approved” outcomes with provider-review status.

## Performance

- Define whether this pathway is wellness education, hormone evaluation, prescription treatment or a combination.
- Identify the laboratory, symptom, medication and medical-history information required for the actual services offered.
- Supply urgent-symptom and specialist-referral rules.

## Sexual Health

- Define the exact services and medications offered for each patient population.
- Supply cardiovascular, medication-interaction, pregnancy, mental-health and urgent-symptom screening appropriate to those services.
- Define when in-person examination, laboratory testing or specialist referral is required.

## Hair Restoration

- Define the offered topical, oral, procedural or compounded options.
- Supply pregnancy, medication, sudden-loss, scalp-condition and referral screening appropriate to those offerings.
- Define photo requirements only after secure uploads are approved.

## Skin Restoration

- Define whether the pathway offers prescription, compounded, cosmetic or wellness services.
- Supply pregnancy, allergy, active-condition, medication and procedural-history screening appropriate to those services.
- Define when photographs or in-person dermatology evaluation are required.

## Required approval record

For every enabled clinical field, record the program and field key, exact visitor-facing wording, clinical purpose, required status, branching behavior, source and source date, approving clinician and approval date, counsel or pharmacy review, and protocol version and effective date.
